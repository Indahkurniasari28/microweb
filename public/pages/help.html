Frequently Asked Questions

How do I connect the Raspberry Pi to MICROWAT? expand_more

Make sure the Raspberry Pi is powered on, connected to the same network as the application, and all cables are securely attached. Open the Device page and verify that the device status is "Connected".

Why is the spectrometer not detected? expand_more

Check the USB connection, restart the spectrometer, and verify that the Avantes driver is installed correctly on the Raspberry Pi.

Why is absorbance data empty? expand_more

Ensure the cuvette is clean, the sample is inside the holder, and the spectrometer has completed calibration.

Why can't I start the reactor? expand_more

Check that the microwave door is closed properly, emergency stop is inactive, and the power supply is connected.

Why is the degradation percentage not changing? expand_more

Verify the photocatalyst concentration, UV lamp condition, microwave power, and irradiation time.

How can I download experiment data? expand_more

Open the History page, choose an experiment session, then click Export to download the data.

How do I change my account password? expand_more

Open the Settings page, select Account Settings, then choose Change Password.

Why is the dashboard showing "Offline"? expand_more

Check your network connection and make sure the Raspberry Pi is online and connected to the server.

Can I use another photocatalyst? expand_more

Yes, but calibration and experiment parameters should be adjusted before running the reactor.

How do I contact the MICROWAT administrator? expand_more

Use the support request form below or contact the administrator through the email provided in the Contact Support section.
